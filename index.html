Surfacing What Users Actually Check

Surfacing What Users Actually Check

Problem

Jupiter Money's home screen shows a new user everything — promotions, Pots, offers — except what the user wants to see their own spend visibility. That's the problem this case study fixes.

Problem

Jupiter Money's home screen shows a new user everything — promotions, Pots, offers — except what the user wants to see their own spend visibility. That's the problem this case study fixes.

A repeating complaint in Jupiter Money's Play Store reviews: the home screen shows everything except your own money. so I started with evidence and desgined.

A repeating complaint in Jupiter Money's Play Store reviews: the home screen shows everything except your own money. so I started with evidence and desgined.

Impact

Balance visibility reduced from 1 tap to 0 taps. Spend total moved from the 4th scrolled element to the first visible card. Pots quick-actions reduced from 4 to 1, a 75% cut in surfaced choices — with full functionality preserved one tap deeper.
